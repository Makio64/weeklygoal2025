Privacy Policy

WeeklyGoal

Last updated: October 26, 2025

Introduction

WeeklyGoal is a weekly goal management application that respects your privacy. This privacy policy explains how we handle your information.

Data Collection

WeeklyGoal does NOT collect ANY personal data. All your information remains stored locally on your device.

The following data is stored only on your device:

  • Your weekly goals
  • Your progress
  • Your notification preferences
  • Your weekly history (last 12 weeks)

Data Storage

All your data is stored locally on your device via Capacitor Preferences. No data is transmitted to external servers, neither to us nor to third parties.

Notifications

The application may send local notifications to remind you of your goals. These notifications are managed entirely on your device and do not require an internet connection.

Data Sharing

We do not share any data with third parties because we do not collect any data. Your information remains private and under your control.

Data Deletion

You can delete all your data at any time by uninstalling the application from your device.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy occasionally. Any changes will be indicated by updating the date at the top of this page.

Contact

For any questions regarding this privacy policy, you can contact us via the store where you downloaded this app.
